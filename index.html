Superconducting Quantum Devices

We design, fabricate and measure superconducting nanoelectronic circuits — from quantum phase slip nanowires to tunable microwave resonators and parametric amplifiers — to build new hardware for quantum information processing and sensing.

What we do

Research highlights

Our work spans the full cycle from device physics and design through nanofabrication to millikelvin microwave measurement.

Quantum phase slip nanowires

Disordered superconducting nanowires in which coherent phase slips provide the exact dual of the Josephson effect.

Microwave resonators & CQUIDs

High-kinetic-inductance NbN resonators and gate-tunable charge-based interferometers.

Parametric amplification

Nonlinear superconducting circuits for low-noise amplification, three-wave mixing and qubit readout.
